3Light mode — inherited default, one-tap override
The app now runs on theme tokens; every screen renders in both modes. Default is inherited, never asked: the app reads prefers-color-scheme — the OS theme is the closest proxy for how Nimiq Pay renders, since the framework exposes only window.nimiqPay.language. The sun/moon button beside the purse chip overrides it, instantly (≤150 ms) and persistently. Only colour changes: identical type, spacing and layout; positions stay words and +/− signs; elevation stays hairline + surface step, no shadows. Amber splits into two tokens — fill #E8940C in both modes (ink text on it), text #E8940C on dark and #A05E00 on light (darkened one step from #A66200, which misses 4.5:1 on #F7F5F2). The light drop-out keeps a higher opacity floor (35%) with the "nets to zero" chip carrying the meaning. Submission screenshots and the thumbnail stay dark. Pairs below are the five break-prone screens: 3a home · 3b collapse · 3c settling · 3d purse step 1 · 3e declined.
