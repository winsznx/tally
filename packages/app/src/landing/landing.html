Netting

Five debts become two transfers.

Tally collapses pairwise debts, cancels cycles exactly, then matches the largest debtor to the largest creditor — at most n−1 transfers for n people, computed the same way on every device. And whoever nets to zero drops out of the round entirely: they never send, never receive, never even open the app.

The purse

Approve once. Never again.

One signature derives a small spending key — the purse — that pays your settlements while Tally is open. Your wallet key is used exactly twice, ever. It is a cash drawer, not a vault: whatever the purse holds is all that can ever be lost, withdraw-all is one tap from every screen, and skipping it is fine forever — you just approve each payment yourself.
